# Aesthetify

Your music. Your atmosphere.

## Install in Chrome

1. Extract this ZIP into a folder.
2. Open `chrome://extensions`, enable **Developer mode**, and choose **Load unpacked**.
3. Select the folder containing `manifest.json`.
4. Open Spotify Web, refresh the tab, choose a song, and press Play.

Keep all older Neon installations removed or disabled. Install only one Aesthetify copy.

## Make it yours

Use **Auto palette** for a color journey, or choose Emerald Gold, Rose Quartz, Midnight Rain, Solar Fire, Violet Dream, Ocean Glow, or Golden Hour. Each palette has its own animated atmosphere.

The matching player dock stays available outside immersive view. The arrow reveals Spotify's original player. Escape closes immersive view. The toolbar icon opens Aesthetify's controls.

**Settings** controls automatic opening and window droplets. **Personal lyrics** optionally imports a timed lyric file. Imported files and preferences stay on your device.

Spotify's visible highlighted line drives the lyric animation. The word pops are visual transitions, not isolated-vocal timing. When a clear current line is unavailable or Spotify shows an instrumental marker, ambient music visuals appear instead. Songs without available lyrics cannot gain lyrics through Aesthetify.

Spotify remains responsible for music playback. Aesthetify does not capture, analyze, record or reroute audio. The small glow above the lyrics is decorative.
